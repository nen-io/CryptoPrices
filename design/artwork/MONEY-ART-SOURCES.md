# Money-related artwork for the design study

Verified and downloaded on 2026-09-17. These files come directly from museum image services. No stock-image search, unverified reproduction, paid licence, remote font or generated imitation was used. The original compositions were retained; the museum IIIF services supplied versions with a maximum dimension of 1,800 pixels.

## 1. The Merchant and his Wife

- **Museum title:** *Købmanden med sin kone*; English rendering: *The Merchant and his Wife*. The museum also records the earlier Danish title *Skatteopkræveren med sin kone* (The Tax Collector and his Wife).
- **Artist:** Marinus van Reymerswale, using the museum's spelling.
- **Date / medium:** 1540; oil on wood panel.
- **Collection / accession:** SMK – National Gallery of Denmark, Copenhagen; KMSsp334.
- **Canonical record:** <https://open.smk.dk/artwork/image/KMSsp334>
- **Official machine-readable record:** <https://api.smk.dk/api/v1/art/?object_number=KMSsp334>

- **Local image:** `reymerswale-merchant-and-wife-1540.jpg`, 1800 × 1351 pixels.
- **Rights:** Public domain; [Creative Commons Public Domain Mark 1.0](https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/mark/1.0/). The current museum API explicitly returns `"public_domain": true` and that exact `rights` URL for this object. These fields, the artist/date/title and image service identity are preserved in `reymerswale-merchant-and-wife-1540.metadata.json`.
- **Museum reuse policy:** [Works available for free download](https://www.smk.dk/article/vaerker-til-fri-download/) ([English route](https://www.smk.dk/en/article/free-download-of-images/)). The museum's indexed policy text identifies the reproductions with the Public Domain Mark and gives an artist/title/date/museum credit format. The item-specific API flag above is the direct rights evidence for this selected image.
- **Recommended credit:** Marinus van Reymerswale, *The Merchant and his Wife*, 1540. SMK – National Gallery of Denmark, KMSsp334. Public domain.
- **SHA-256:** `c0ce15710971fa2cbd431680d58131eb3ea2830d4ba27b5b3b7cbfaade154758`

This is the strongest colour option: a money-weighing balance, coins, a ledger, ink and account papers are plainly visible. The museum's catalogue interpretation connects the scene with moneylending and a warning against greed; avoid claiming that it celebrates investment success. The landscape composition works as an editorial image beside portfolio totals, or as a clearly labelled crop of the hands, balance and coins.

## Local use

Both JPEGs were opened and visually inspected after download, and their dimensions and hashes were verified. They can be served locally by the disposable study without runtime requests to a museum or image CDN. No production UI or asset pipeline was changed by this research task.
